# JRRZ Project Vision

## Purpose of this document

This document preserves the long-term direction of the Joint Replacement Recovery Zone.

JRRZ aims to become a trusted, patient-centered navigation layer for joint-replacement preparation and recovery. Its defining interaction is simple: visitors select the joint they are concerned about and enter a focused recovery Zone.

## Principles

- patient-readable before technically impressive;
- clinically aligned without unsupported approval claims;
- useful even while future Zones are under construction;
- explicit about evidence, uncertainty, and individual variation;
- compatible with clinician collaboration while preserving separate roles;
- structured enough to support durable updates and validation.

## Development notice

The vision will be refined as patient, surgeon, therapist, and caregiver feedback is documented.
