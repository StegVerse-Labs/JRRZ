The Hip Zone

This live Zone organizes practical, patient-centered education for people preparing for or recovering from hip replacement. It does not replace individualized instructions from a treating care team.

Current status: Foundational public page. Expanded lived-experience notes, clinician-reviewed resources, and joint-specific milestones will be posted as they are developed and reviewed.

Before surgery

  • Prepare the home for safe movement and commonly used mobility aids.
  • Ask the surgical team which movement precautions apply to the planned procedure.
  • Arrange transportation, support, medications, and follow-up logistics in advance.

Early recovery

  • Follow the surgeon's wound, medication, activity, and warning-sign instructions.
  • Use assistive devices and rehabilitation exercises exactly as directed.
  • Expect progress to vary from day to day; track function and safety rather than comparing one recovery to another.

Functional rebuilding

  • Work toward safe walking mechanics, balance, transfers, and daily activities.
  • Continue strengthening and mobility work according to the rehabilitation plan.
  • Raise new pain, swelling, instability, fever, wound changes, breathing difficulty, or other concerns promptly with the care team.
