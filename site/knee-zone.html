The Knee Zone

This live Zone organizes practical, patient-centered education for people preparing for or recovering from knee replacement. It does not replace individualized instructions from a treating care team.

Current status: Foundational public page. Expanded content, patient perspectives, and clinician-reviewed resources will be posted as collaboration develops.

Before surgery

  • Prepare safe walking routes, seating, bathroom access, and recovery support at home.
  • Discuss expected rehabilitation, assistive devices, medications, and follow-up appointments with the care team.
  • Record questions about swelling, motion, pain control, sleep, and return to daily activities.

Early recovery

  • Follow wound, medication, activity, and warning-sign instructions from the surgical team.
  • Perform prescribed movement and rehabilitation exercises without substituting generic online targets for individual guidance.
  • Track meaningful functions such as safe transfers, walking tolerance, and daily activities.

Functional rebuilding

  • Build strength, balance, confidence, and walking quality according to the rehabilitation plan.
  • Expect recovery to vary; a universal milestone is not a promise for an individual patient.
  • Report concerning symptoms, wound changes, fever, breathing difficulty, sudden swelling, or other urgent changes to the care team.
