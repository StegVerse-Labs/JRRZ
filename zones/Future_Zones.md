# Future Recovery Zones

## Purpose of this document

This document records the status vocabulary and planned expansion of JRRZ beyond hip and knee recovery.

## Canonical status vocabulary

- **Live Zone — blue:** patient-centered guidance is available now.
- **In Recovery — teal:** a dedicated Zone is being prepared; an estimated month/year is published only when confirmed.
- **In Surgery — gray:** research and planning are underway.

## Current map

- Shoulders — In Recovery
- Elbows — In Recovery
- Wrists — In Recovery
- Spine/Neck — In Surgery
- Ankles — In Surgery

## Development notice

Zone names, launch estimates, reviewer records, and joint-specific content will be posted as each workstream receives a durable owner and evidence-backed readiness state.
