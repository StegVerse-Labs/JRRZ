# The Knee Zone

## Purpose of this document

This document defines the scope of the Knee Zone within JRRZ.

The Knee Zone focuses on patient-centered education for preparation, swelling and symptom awareness, movement and walking, rehabilitation participation, strength rebuilding, and long-term joint health after knee replacement.
