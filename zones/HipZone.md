# The Hip Zone

## Purpose of this document

This document defines the scope of the Hip Zone within JRRZ.

The Hip Zone focuses on patient-centered education for preparation, early recovery, walking and balance, daily-life adaptation, strength rebuilding, and long-term joint health after hip replacement.
