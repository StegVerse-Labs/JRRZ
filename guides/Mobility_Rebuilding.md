# Mobility Rebuilding

## Purpose of this document

This guide will organize educational material for rebuilding practical movement after joint replacement.

## Planned coverage

- walking mechanics and assistive-device progression;
- transfers, stairs, and daily activities;
- balance and fall prevention;
- strength, endurance, and pacing;
- communicating plateaus or unexpected changes.

## Development notice

Detailed, cited, joint-specific guidance will be posted soon after clinical review and source verification.
