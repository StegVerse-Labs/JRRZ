# Early Recovery

## Purpose of this document

This guide will organize educational topics for the first days and weeks after joint replacement.

## Planned coverage

- following discharge instructions;
- wound and symptom awareness;
- safe movement and assistive devices;
- rehabilitation participation;
- sleep, fatigue, and pacing;
- when to contact the treating team.

## Development notice

Detailed, cited, and reviewer-attributed guidance will be posted soon. This guide does not replace individualized medical instructions.
