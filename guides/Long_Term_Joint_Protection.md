# Long-Term Joint Protection

## Purpose of this document

This guide will organize educational topics for maintaining function and protecting a replaced joint over time.

## Planned coverage

- sustainable strength and mobility habits;
- follow-up and change monitoring;
- activity decisions and implant protection;
- fall prevention and whole-body conditioning;
- questions to raise when function changes.
