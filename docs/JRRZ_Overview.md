# JRRZ Overview

## Purpose of this document

This document defines the scope and intended use of the Joint Replacement Recovery Zone.

JRRZ is a patient-centered educational framework for people preparing for, undergoing, and recovering from joint replacement. It organizes recovery into understandable phases while preserving the authority of each patient's surgeon, physician, and rehabilitation team.

## Current scope

- The Hip Zone
- The Knee Zone
- Patient Recovery Roadmap
- Recovery Trajectory Model
- Systems-thinking comparison
- Future joint-specific zones

## Development notice

Expanded patient stories, clinician-reviewed resources, and joint-specific references will be posted as they are developed and reviewed.
