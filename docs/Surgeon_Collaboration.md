# Surgeon Collaboration

## Purpose of this document

This document defines how JRRZ may receive and represent contributions from orthopedic surgeons, physical therapists, rehabilitation professionals, and other qualified reviewers.

## Collaboration principles

- Patient education remains distinct from individual medical advice.
- Contributor names, credentials, scope, and approval status must be documented before endorsement language is used.
- Clinician review does not automatically imply organizational sponsorship.
- Corrections and conflicts must be traceable to the affected page or resource.

## Potential contributions

- pre-operative expectation guidance;
- common recovery questions;
- warning-sign and escalation education;
- review of joint-specific milestone language;
- patient-facing explanations of rehabilitation concepts.

## Development notice

A formal reviewer record, contribution agreement, and approval ledger will be posted before JRRZ makes any claim that content is orthopedic-approved or formally endorsed.
